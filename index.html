WHY LOK EXISTS

Preserving what
makes us, us.

Local heritage is more than monuments. It is the knowledge, creativity and memory carried by communities.

THE IDEA

A shared home for local memory.

Community stories, traditions, photographs and historical records can remain scattered across private collections. When these fragments are difficult to find or pass on, valuable knowledge risks being forgotten.

LOK imagines a simple, organized digital space where contributors can document cultural materials, add context and attribution, and help others discover connected memories.
